## Full description (4,000 characters)

Setward is a free workout log for people who lift. Log your sets in seconds, see last time's numbers beside every set, and watch your records and progress build up. It works offline, needs no account, shows no ads and keeps your workouts on your phone.

LOG FASTER
• Weight, reps, time or distance, with warm-up, failure and drop sets, RPE, notes and supersets
• Your previous numbers sit next to every set
• A rest timer starts when you check a set off, with sounds, vibration and an alert that works with the screen off
• Forgot a session? Add it afterwards, on its own date

ROUTINES THAT FIT YOU
• Build your own, save any past workout as a routine, or start from one of six starter programs: full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs, body-part split, machines and cables, dumbbells at home
• Group routines in folders and put them in your order

1,000+ EXERCISES
• Pictures and instructions for most
• Add your own and choose what you log
• A plate calculator that uses the plates your gym really has

SEE YOUR PROGRESS
• Personal records, and your latest ones at a glance
• A chart for every exercise: heaviest weight, estimated 1RM, volume
• Weekly time, volume and sets, sets per muscle, a consistency calendar, a weekly goal and streak
• Body-weight tracking

YOURS TO KEEP
• Your data stays on your device. Back it up to a file, or sync it to your own private GitHub repository
• Bring your history over from Strong, or export everything as CSV
• Free, with no account, no ads and no tracking
• Open source (MIT)

MAKE IT YOURS
• Eleven languages, including right-to-left Arabic (translations other than English are machine-made)
• kg or lb, km or mi, 12 or 24-hour time, dark, light or pure black, accent colours, large text, high contrast and larger controls

Setward is a web app that you install from your browser. Exercise names and instructions are in English.
